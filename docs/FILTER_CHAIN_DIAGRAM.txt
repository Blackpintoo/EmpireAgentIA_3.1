================================================================================
PROBLEM ANALYSIS: WHY ZERO TRADES?
================================================================================

Multiple filters working in SEQUENCE means EACH ONE is a potential blocker.

With 34+ filters, the probability of a trade passing ALL is EXTREMELY LOW.

Estimated blocking probability:
  If each filter has 90% pass rate:
    0.9^34 = 3% chance of trade passing ALL filters
    (Realistic: 0.5-1% chance)

CRITICAL CHOKE POINTS (in order of severity):

1. TIER 2 (Quality Gates) - Blocks ~80-90% of signals
   └─ min_score: 8.0 is VERY high (standard: 2-3)
   └─ min_confluence: 5 is VERY high (standard: 2-3)
   └─ weighted_threshold: 3.0 is restrictive

2. TIER 3 (Time Filters) - Blocks ~60% of time
   └─ Global hours: 12 hours blocked per day
   └─ Low-liquidity: 14 more hours avoided
   └─ Symbol-specific: Some only 2-3 hours available

3. TIER 4 (Market Filters) - Blocks ~30-50%
   └─ MTF confluence: 70% alignment very restrictive
   └─ Sentiment extremes: Rare
   └─ Regime detection: May contradict signal

4. TIER 6 (Cooldown) - Blocks recovery
   └─ 15-min cooldown after every trade
   └─ 60-min cooldown after losses
   └─ 120-min cooldown after 3 losses (2-hour lock!)

5. TIER 7 (Kill Switch) - Stops everything
   └─ If daily loss > $400, ALL trading stops

   └─ No, 15:00 is OPEN ✓

5. Score ≥ 8.0?
   └─ Requires VERY strong signal (rare)

6. Confluence ≥ 5?
   └─ Requires 5+ agents aligned (rare)

7. Weighted score ≥ 3.0?
   └─ Restrictive threshold

8. MTF confluence ≥ 0.7?
   └─ 70% of timeframes must align

9. Open positions < 2?
   └─ Depends on current state

10. No cooldown active?
    └─ Depends on recent trades

11. No loss cooldown?
    └─ Depends on recent losses

12. Daily loss < $400?
    └─ Depends on day's P&L

RESULT: Even if time is acceptable, must pass 9+ additional quality/risk checks
        Probability: <5% for each proposal

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

To increase trade execution rate, prioritize relaxing (in order):

1. Min score threshold (lower from 8.0 to 6.0 or 5.0)
2. Min confluence (lower from 5 to 3 or 4)
3. Weighted voting threshold (lower from 3.0 to 2.0)
4. MTF confluence ratio (lower from 0.7 to 0.6)
5. Session hour filters (enable more trading hours)
6. Cooldown periods (reduce from 15→10 min, 60→30 min)
7. Symbol-specific hour restrictions (whitelist fewer hours)

Approach: Relax ONE parameter at a time and test.
